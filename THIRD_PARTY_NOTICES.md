updater implementations and retained Rec Room artwork. RecFlare logos are
provided separately in `assets`. Original notices and attributions in source
files are retained; this document does not relicense third-party material.

software, not newly reverse engineered implementations. Their license notices
remain in dependency sources and packaged runtime/license files. esbuild emits
bundled JavaScript license comments alongside the bundles.

The Windows installer temporarily includes upstream 7-Zip and its license.
Unity, Unity Hub, Studio templates and SDK packages are obtained separately.
